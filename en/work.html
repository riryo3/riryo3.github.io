Works

  • DialMatrix Settings app for Rev-O-mate (Mac / Windows)

    A desktop app for changing a device's settings from a computer. It backs up the device before writing. Works on Mac and Windows.

  • Potalker Client for a web voice-chat service (Mac / Windows / Linux / Android)

    An unofficial client that makes a browser voice-chat service usable as an app. The Mac version is written in Swift and the Windows version in C# (WPF / WebView2). Verified on Mac, Windows, Linux and Android.

  • Jikoku (Time Announcer) iPhone app that speaks the time (App Store)

    An iPhone app that announces the time by voice at 15, 30 or 60 minute intervals. You can choose the hours, voice, speed and reading style. It collects no personal data and has no ads. Free on the App Store.

  • Pochitto Talk (alpha) Walkie-talkie style group call app (Web)

    A web app for group voice calls where you talk only while holding the button. Works in the browser with no install.

  • Handmade Listing Price Calculator A small business tool for pricing listings (Web)

    Calculates a listing price from material costs, work time, selling fees and shipping. Also shows target profit and profit after discounts.

  • Ultimate Word Chain A word-chain puzzle with real-time matches (Web)

    A word-chain puzzle where longer overlaps earn more points. Play solo or in real-time matches with friends.
